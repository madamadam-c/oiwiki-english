Mo's algorithm was proposed by Mo Tao. Before Mo Tao proposed it, the algorithm had already circulated on a small scale among high-level Codeforces users, but Mo Tao was the first to summarize it in detail. When Mo Tao introduced the algorithm, he only analyzed the ordinary version, but through the collective wisdom of OIers and ACMers, Mo's algorithm gained many extended variants.

Mo's algorithm can solve a class of offline range-query problems and is extremely broadly applicable. With extensions, it can also easily handle path queries on trees and operations with modifications.
